Sheep Loop

Guide colourful sheep around the loop and sort them into matching pens.

About

Tap a sheep and it joins the flock on the loop. Guide each colour into its matching pen and fill the pen to send the flock home. The loop only has room for so many sheep at once, so look at the front of each queue, check which pens have space and decide who goes next.

What is in it

  • 100+ levels of colour sorting around a loop with limited space
  • Fill a pen with one colour to clear it and make room for the rest of the flock
  • New challenges as you play: Hidden Wool, Mystery Sheep, Barn Doors, Linked Sheep, Gates and Color Pens
  • Boosters for tricky boards: Extra Space, Shuffle, Hand and Mallet
  • Daily rewards and coins, free to play
