Wild Paws

Role Playing · Android

Every run is a chapter of days: a fight, a choice, a camp, a merchant or a pact. Build a squad of three animal heroes, survive to the last day and beat the boss.

  • Animal heroes, each with their own role in the squad
  • A different path through the jungle every run
  • A boss waiting at the end of each chapter
