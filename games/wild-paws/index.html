Wild Paws

Lead a squad of animal heroes through the jungle in a roguelike RPG.

About

Every run is a chapter of days. Each day brings a fight, a choice, a camp, a merchant, a lucky wheel or a pact with an angel or a demon. Survive to the last day and beat the boss.

What is in it

  • Three animal heroes to a squad, across front and back slots
  • Tanks, warriors, rangers, assassins and supports, with fire, water, nature, light and dark countering each other
  • 3v3 auto battles: heroes clash and fire their ultimates while the next move gets planned
  • Perk drafts during a run, so no two squads end up the same
  • Hero summons, star ranks and gear between runs
  • Free to play, in English and Vietnamese
