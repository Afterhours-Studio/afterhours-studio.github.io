Slide Pop

Slide colourful cubes, match three and clear the board before time runs out.

Screenshots

About

Swipe a jelly cube and it slides until something stops it. Line up three of the same colour and they pop. Clear the board before the clock runs out. Easy to learn, tricky to master.

What is in it

  • Hundreds of hand-tuned levels, every one solvable
  • Hard and Super Hard tiers for players who want the squeeze
  • Obstacles: ice blocks, rocks, cages, metal boxes, arrow tiles, glue and numbered cubes
  • Boosters: Hammer, Freeze, UFO and Shuffle
  • A journey from Cube Meadow to the Crystal Castle
  • 3D jelly cubes, relaxing music and haptic feedback
  • Plays offline, free to play
