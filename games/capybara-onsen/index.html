Capybara Onsen

Soak cute capybaras and grow a relaxing hot spring onsen.

About

Capybaras love a hot soak. Let them relax in the spring, earn coins while they bathe, and spend those coins turning a small pool into a hot spring resort. The game keeps earning while it is closed, so there is nothing to miss.

What is in it

  • Idle earnings that keep running offline
  • Dozens of buildings, from the Onsen Hut to the Sauna, each changing look as it upgrades
  • A farm and an orchard: carrots, sunflowers, corn, pumpkins, yuzu and mandarins
  • Hats and necklaces for every capybara
  • Friendships, visiting animals, pool toys and Hana's tasks
  • Seasonal events, daily login chests and a lucky wheel
  • A day and night sky that follows the seasons
  • Free to play, in English and Vietnamese
