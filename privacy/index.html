Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Afterhours Studio (“we”, “us”) handles information in connection with the mobile games and applications we publish (each, the “Game”). It applies to all games published by Afterhours Studio on Google Play, unless a specific Game links to a different policy.

Summary

  • We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not ask for your name, email address, phone number or payment details.
  • Our Games display advertising and use crash and usage analytics. These are provided by Google.
  • Those services automatically collect technical identifiers and usage data, described in detail below.
  • We do not sell your personal information.

Information collected

We do not operate our own servers and do not collect data directly. The data below is collected automatically by third-party software development kits (SDKs) embedded in our Games.

Google AdMob (advertising)

DataPurpose
Advertising ID (a resettable device identifier)Serving and measuring ads, limiting repeated ads, fraud prevention
Device information — model, operating system version, language, screen size, network typeDelivering ads that render correctly
Approximate location, derived from IP address (typically country or region level)Showing region-appropriate ads and complying with local law
Ad interaction events — impressions, clicksMeasuring ad performance and calculating revenue

Google Firebase Analytics (usage analytics)

DataPurpose
App instance identifierDistinguishing one installation from another
In-game events — levels started and completed, screens viewed, session length, first openUnderstanding which parts of the Game are used, and improving them
Device and app information — model, OS version, app version, language, countryDiagnosing problems specific to a device or region

Google Firebase Crashlytics (stability)

DataPurpose
Crash reports — stack traces, exception type, the moment the crash occurredFinding and fixing crashes
Crashlytics installation identifierGrouping multiple crashes from the same installation
Device state at the time of the crash — model, OS version, available memory and storage, orientationReproducing the conditions that caused the crash

How the information is used

  • To display advertising, which is how our Games are funded.
  • To measure whether advertising works and to detect invalid or fraudulent traffic.
  • To understand how our Games are played, so we can balance difficulty and improve them.
  • To identify, reproduce and fix crashes and performance problems.

We do not use this data to build a profile of you as an individual, and we do not attempt to identify you by name.

Sharing

Data collected by the SDKs listed above is transmitted to Google LLC.

  • Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics process data on our behalf, as our service provider.
  • AdMob also uses data for its own advertising purposes, including serving personalised ads across its network where you have consented to that.

Google handling of this data is governed by the Google Privacy Policy. See also how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Beyond this, we do not share data with any third party, except where we are legally required to do so.

Your choices

  • Reset or delete your Advertising ID. On Android: Settings › Privacy › Ads. Deleting the Advertising ID stops personalised advertising on your device across all apps.
  • Opt out of personalised ads. The same Android settings screen offers this option. You will still see ads, but they will not be based on your interests.
  • Consent in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland. Where required, our Games show a consent request before any advertising identifier is used for personalisation, via the Google User Messaging Platform. You may change or withdraw your choice from the Game settings or privacy screen.
  • Uninstall. Removing the Game stops all further collection.

Data deletion requests

Our Games do not have user accounts, so there is no account to delete. To request deletion of data associated with your device, email h1dr0n@icloud.com with:

  • the name of the Game,
  • your Advertising ID, or the approximate date and time you played, and
  • your device model and Android version.

We will acknowledge the request within 30 days and forward it to Google for deletion of the associated records. You can delete your Advertising ID yourself at any time from Android settings, which takes effect immediately.

Children

Our Games are intended for players aged 13 and over, and are not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13, or under the equivalent minimum age in your country. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Retention

We do not store this data ourselves. Retention is controlled by Google: Firebase Analytics event data is retained for a configurable period and is deleted automatically afterwards; Crashlytics reports are retained for up to 90 days; AdMob data is retained according to the Google retention schedule.

Security

All data described here is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS). Because we do not host this data on our own infrastructure, it is stored on Google systems and protected by Google security measures.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or restrict the processing of your personal data, to object to processing, and to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. Residents of California may have the right to know what personal information is collected and to request its deletion. We do not sell personal information. To exercise any of these rights, use the contact address below.

Where consent is the legal basis (personalised advertising), you may withdraw it at any time. Where our legitimate interest is the basis (crash reporting, aggregate analytics, fraud prevention), you may object.

Changes

We may update this policy when our Games change or when the law requires it. The “Last updated” date at the top of the page will change. Substantial changes will be announced in the Game or in its Google Play listing.
