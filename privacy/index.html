Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Afterhours Studio (“we”, “us”) handles information in connection with the mobile games and applications we publish (each, the “Game”). It applies to all games published by Afterhours Studio on Google Play, unless a specific Game links to a different policy.

For the purposes of data protection law, Afterhours Studio is the data controller for the information described below. By installing and playing a Game you agree to the handling of information set out in this policy. If you do not agree with it, please do not install our Games.

Summary

  • We do not ask you to create an account with us, and we do not ask for your name, email address, phone number or payment details.
  • Some of our Games offer an optional Google Play Games sign-in, with cloud save to your own Google account. It is never required, and the Game works without it.
  • Our Games display advertising, offer optional in-app purchases, and use crash and usage analytics. All of these are provided by Google.
  • Those services automatically collect technical identifiers and usage data, described in detail below.
  • We do not sell your personal information for money. Advertising identifiers are made available to Google for personalised advertising, which some US state laws treat as “sharing”. You can opt out at any time, as described below.

Google Play Games (sign-in and cloud save)

Some of our Games offer an optional Google Play Games sign-in. It is never required: you can decline it, or simply not use it, and the Game plays the same way without it. If you have signed in before, the Game may sign you in again silently on the next launch, using the account you already approved. You can sign out at any time, as described in Your choices.

DataPurpose
Play Games player ID (an identifier for your Play Games profile, specific to our Games)Recognising you as the signed-in player, and matching you with your cloud save
Play Games gamer tag (the display name you chose for Play Games)Showing who is signed in, on the Game screen
Saved game data (progress, levels and areas unlocked, in-game currencies and items, settings)Backing up your progress, and restoring it on a new device or after reinstalling

The player ID and the gamer tag stay on your device and are only used to show, inside the Game, who is signed in. We do not send them to Firebase Analytics or to Crashlytics, and they do not appear in crash reports: those services use their own identifiers, which are not linked to your Google account.

While you are signed in, the Game uploads your saved game data to the Google Play Games Saved Games service, into storage that belongs to your own Google account. This happens when you leave the Game running in the background, and every few minutes while you play. We cannot read, change or delete that data: it is held by Google under your account, and the Game reads it back only on a device where you are signed in. When you sign in on a device that already holds local progress, the Game compares the two saves and asks you which one to keep before anything is overwritten.

How the information is used

  • To display advertising, which is how our Games are funded.
  • To measure whether advertising works and to detect invalid or fraudulent traffic.
  • To understand how our Games are played, so we can balance difficulty and improve them.
  • To identify, reproduce and fix crashes and performance problems.
  • To save your progress to your own Google account and restore it later, where you have signed in to Google Play Games.

We do not use this data to build a profile of you as an individual, and we do not attempt to identify you by name.

Sharing

Data collected by the SDKs listed above is transmitted to Google LLC.

  • Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics process data on our behalf, as our service provider.
  • AdMob also uses data for its own advertising purposes, including serving personalised ads across its network where you have consented to that.
  • Google Play Games, where you sign in, provides your player ID and gamer tag to the Game and holds your saved game data in your own Google account. We do not have access to that storage, and we do not pass your player ID or gamer tag to anyone.

Google handling of this data is governed by the Google Privacy Policy. See also how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Beyond this, we do not share data with any third party, except in the following cases:

  • Legal process. Where we believe in good faith that disclosure is required by law, by a court order, subpoena or other legal process, or by a request from a competent authority.
  • Safety and fraud prevention. Where disclosure is necessary to investigate suspected fraud or abuse, to enforce our terms, or to protect the rights, property or safety of our players, of us, or of the public.
  • Business transfer. If Afterhours Studio is merged, acquired, or sells a Game or its assets, information may be transferred to the acquiring party. It will remain subject to this policy, and we will announce the change on this page beforehand where it is practical to do so.

International transfers

Where data originating in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland is transferred outside those territories, the transfer relies on the safeguards Google puts in place for its own services, including the European Commission Standard Contractual Clauses and, where applicable, the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework. Your choices

  • Reset or delete your Advertising ID. On Android: Settings › Privacy › Ads. Deleting the Advertising ID stops personalised advertising on your device across all apps.
  • Opt out of personalised ads. The same Android settings screen offers this option. You will still see ads, but they will not be based on your interests.
  • Consent in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland. Where required, our Games show a consent request before any advertising identifier is used for personalisation, via the Google User Messaging Platform. You may change or withdraw your choice from the Game settings or privacy screen.
  • Industry opt-out tools. These are operated by third parties, not by us: Network Advertising Initiative, Digital Advertising Alliance, Your Online Choices (EU), and Google Ads Settings.
  • Google Play Games sign-in. Signing in is optional. You can decline it when a Game offers it, and you can sign out from the Game at any time. Once you sign out, the Game stops uploading your progress and continues from the save held on your device.
  • Uninstall. Removing the Game stops all further collection.

If you opt out of personalised advertising you will still see ads in our Games, but they will not be selected on the basis of your activity in other apps or websites.

Data deletion requests

Our Games do not have user accounts, so there is no account to delete. To request deletion of data associated with your device, email h1dr0n@icloud.com with:

  • the name of the Game,
  • your Advertising ID, or the approximate date and time you played, and
  • your device model and Android version.

We will acknowledge the request within 30 days and forward it to Google for deletion of the associated records. You can delete your Advertising ID yourself at any time from Android settings, which takes effect immediately.

If you have used the Google Play Games sign-in, your cloud save is held in your own Google account and we can neither read nor delete it, so we cannot do this part for you. To remove it, sign out in the Game, then delete that Game’s saved data from your Google account: open the Google Play Games app, go to Settings › Delete Play Games account & data, choose to delete individual game data, and select the Game. Deleting the cloud save does not remove the progress stored on your device; uninstalling the Game does that.
