The studio

Made in Hanoi, from first sketch to store listing.

Afterhours Studio is a game development team in Hanoi, Vietnam. We handle every part of a game ourselves: art, code, balance, live updates and the release work that follows.

Our games are built for short, calm sessions: something to pick up after a long day and put down feeling better.
